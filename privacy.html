Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

What we collect

Old School Apps may collect account details, app data you choose to sync, payment status, feedback, and basic usage analytics. We do not send note text, movie reviews, passwords, or payment card numbers to Google Analytics or Meta Pixel.

How we use information

We use information to provide accounts, cloud sync, subscriptions, downloads, support, security, and product analytics. Stripe processes payments; Supabase provides authentication and cloud storage; Vercel hosts the website.

Analytics and choices

Optional Google Analytics and Meta Pixel tracking is loaded only after consent. Meta Pixel helps measure advertising visits and conversions. You can decline analytics in the banner or clear the site’s analytics-consent storage in your browser.

Retention and requests

We retain account and billing records as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Contact support@old-school-apps.com for access, correction, or deletion requests.
